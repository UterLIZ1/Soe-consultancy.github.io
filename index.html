01 ABOUT SOE

A CONNECTED APPROACH TO HIRING

People are the solution
to every business challenge.

SOE stands for Solution of Everything. We support organisations by helping them find professionals who match their specific skills, experience and role requirements.

As a third-party recruitment and staffing vendor, we work with client companies that share their hiring requirements with us. We then identify and screen relevant candidates and coordinate the next steps with the client.

03 HOW IT WORKS

A STRAIGHTFORWARD PROCESS

From requirement
to selection.

We coordinate each stage so the client can meet relevant candidates and candidates can understand the opportunity before moving forward.

01

Client shares a requirement

The client provides the role details, required skills, experience and other relevant expectations.

↘
02

SOE identifies suitable candidates

We look for candidates whose skills and experience align with the shared requirement.

↘
03

We discuss fit and expectations

We speak with candidates about their experience, interest in the role and salary expectations.

↘
04

Candidate is submitted to the client

With the candidate's agreement, we share the profile for the client's review and interview process.

↘
05

Interview, selection and agreed terms

The client conducts its interview and selection process. If selected, the commercial and payment arrangements are handled as agreed between the relevant parties.
